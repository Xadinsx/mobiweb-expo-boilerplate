# NNNN. Short title

- Status: proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN
- Date: YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What problem or choice made this necessary? Include the constraints.

## Decision

What we chose, in one or two sentences.

## Alternatives

What else we considered and why we did not choose it.

## Consequences

What gets easier, what gets harder, and what we must now do or watch.

## Deviation variant

For declining a recommended default, name the file `NNNN-deviation-<capability>.md` and use these sections in place of Decision and Alternatives:

- **Recommended default:** the module in `docs/modules/` and its default.
- **Chosen instead:** what this project uses, or "removed".
- **Reason:** why the default does not fit this client.
- **Steps taken:** which steps of the module's manifest were done, and any that were not.
- **Rules and checks that now differ:** what changed in the module's rules, checks and leftover checks. The manifest and the module index status are updated to match.
